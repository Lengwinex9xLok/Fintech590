Data and model design

Coverage and label construction

Data scope and lineage

The unit is a Call Report filing bank identified by RSSD ID and quarter, not its parent holding company. The committed ingested panel contains 239,553 bank-quarters for 6,581 banks across 46 quarters from 2015 Q1 to 2026 Q2. Stored reconciliation differences by form are zero. Counts alone do not validate individual values; raw CDR ZIPs were not independently re-ingested in this review. [S5, S6]

Current team defined stress triggers

TriggerResearch assumption
Tier 1 leverageBelow 7.0 percent
Noncurrent loans divided by total loansAbove 3.0 percent
Annualized net income divided by quarter-end assetsBelow negative 0.5 percent

Any trigger defines a breach. The team retains these research cutoffs to define capital, credit and earnings deterioration; they are not regulatory standards or demonstrated optimal choices. Alternative target thresholds have not been tested. Banks already in breach at the scoring quarter are shown separately from the forward-risk watchlist. [S1, S2]

Corrected four quarter labels

A positive requires an observed breach in one of the next four calendar quarters. A negative requires all four future quarters to be filed without a breach. Otherwise the outcome is unresolved. Matching by quarter-end date prevents a filing gap from shifting the window. Recomputing labels from the committed ingested panel matched every exported target; no negative had incomplete follow-up. The model excludes the future-observation count from its inputs. [S2, S3, S8]

Training and evaluation

The model is fitted on 94,339 resolved bank-quarters from 2017 to 2021, with a 2022 gap year and 33,911 development-evaluation observations from 2023 to 2024. XGBoost reweights the positive class. Variants are compared and the score cutoff is chosen on that evaluation period, so it is not an untouched final holdout. Source features use the scoring quarter or earlier, but historical reporting vintages were not reconstructed. [S1, S3, S4]

Use the actual ratio definitions

The feature called nim is annualized NII divided by quarter-end total assets. The dashboard labels it NII to total assets because standard NIM uses average earning assets. ROA likewise uses quarter-end rather than average assets. CRE to capital uses a Tier 1 fallback when total risk-based capital is missing. Disclose these proxies when comparing banks. [S2, S7]

Model selection is a tradeoff

We retain the working deduplicated model to reduce repeated versions of the same ratios and preserve broader driver coverage, accepting lower average precision. Its quarterly top 50 precision differs from naive by one hit across 400 reviews. This is not evidence of statistical equivalence or superior prediction. The metric labeled PR AUC in the source is sklearn average precision, not trapezoidal area. [S3, S8]

Keep review policies distinct

A fixed cutoff of 0.909748 flags 400 observations across eight evaluation quarters: precision 40.75 percent and recall 7.53 percent. It averages 50 reviews per quarter but does not cap each quarter. Selecting exactly the top 50 each quarter gives precision 42.25 percent and recall 7.80 percent. Both use a 6.39 percent base rate. The capacity of 50 remains a team assumption. [S3, S4, S8]

Review BofA rather than assuming distress

At 2026 Q2, BofA NA has score 0.9141, rank 54 and leverage 7.1144 percent. Leverage is its largest positive SHAP contribution, although it is above the current 7 percent breach trigger. The 7 percent target trigger and 0.909748 score cutoff serve different purposes. Its flag makes deposit coverage highly concentrated; this calls for case review, not a failure claim. Changing a threshold and retraining is still needed to establish target-threshold sensitivity. [S1, S8]

Explain scores with the correct model

Global SHAP describes the full candidate model; local SHAP explains the final 12-feature model in log-odds. Neither establishes causality. Automated reconstruction of 35,573 local scores matched exports within 0.00000075. Model fitting was not independently rerun. [S3, S8]
