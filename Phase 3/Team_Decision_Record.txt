Model: ews-xgb-0.2 (pipeline commit 05b38f7)

Both team members agreed to keep the settings below for the Phase 3
submission. These are project choices for a course early warning screen,
not regulatory standards or settings approved for live use.

1. Stress target
A bank is in breach if any one of these holds:
  - Tier 1 leverage ratio below 7.0%
  - Noncurrent loans above 3.0% of total loans
  - Annualized net income below negative 0.5% of quarter end assets
The model predicts whether a bank not already in breach crosses one of
these lines within the next four calendar quarters. The target measures
capital, credit and earnings deterioration. It does not measure bank
failure or deposit runs. We have not tested other cutoffs, so we do not
claim these are optimal.

2. Feature set
We keep the deduplicated 12 feature model. It drops near duplicate
versions of the same ratio and keeps a wider spread of drivers on the
dashboard. The naive top 12 scored higher on average precision (0.221
vs 0.208); we accept that tradeoff and do not claim the deduplicated
model predicts better.

3. Review budget and score cutoff
The score cutoff is 0.910, chosen so that about 50 banks a quarter were
flagged in the 2023 to 2024 evaluation period. Fifty is our assumed
review budget, not a hard cap. In 2026 Q2 the model flags 61 banks: the
top 50 are priority reviews and the other 11 stay visible as overflow.
Banks already in breach are listed separately. Scores rank banks; they
are not calibrated probabilities.

4. Override policy
A reviewer who did not build the model may propose adding or removing a
bank, with dated evidence. The committee chair approves. The original
score and flag are never changed; the override, its reason and the
evidence are logged. If overrides affect more than 20% of the original
flagged list in a quarter, the model is escalated for review. This 20%
line is our own rule.
