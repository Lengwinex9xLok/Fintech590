JPMorgan Chase is the most exposed bank in the peer group. Its 12 month cumulative liquidity gap is 29.7% of total assets, 7 to 10 points below every peer (36.3% to 39.5%), because liabilities repricing within a year equal 16.4% of assets versus 8.8% to 11.6% for the other four banks. This is a funding problem, not a capital problem: its Tier 1 capital ratio of 15.1% is the highest in the group and its leverage ratio of 6.6% is in line with peers, so the committee should prioritize a review of JPMorgan's short term liability mix and deposit repricing assumptions rather than any capital action.
